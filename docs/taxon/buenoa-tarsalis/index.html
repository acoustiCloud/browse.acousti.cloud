What the sources say about this taxon, as written.

diagnosis · Plazi

Diagnosis. Medium to large species, male length 6.2–7.2 mm . Synthlipsis wide, ~ 1 / 2 of vertex width. Labial prong longer than third labial article, originating proximally on article. Fore femur with apex narrowed; stridulatory area oblong, with 17–23 ridges. First article of middle tarsus deeply emarginated.
