comparisons · Plazi

Comparisons. Based on both morphological and molecular evidence, Gekko tesselatus sp. nov. is closely related to G. fengshanensis , G. kwangsiensis , G. liboensis , and G. paucituberculatus . Detailed morphological comparisons between Gekko tesselatus sp. nov. and four closest species are shown in Table 5 and Fig. 10 . Gekko tesselatus sp. nov. can be distinguished from G. fengshanensis by (1) MBSR 98–106 (vs. 149–161), (2) VS 31–39 (vs. 40–49), (3) SMC 130–157 (vs. 197–213), (4) DTR 12–15 (vs. 9–11), (5) PP 6–8 (vs. 9–12), (6) LF 1 7–10 (vs. 11–13), (7) LT 1 7–9 (vs. 12–14), and (8) LT 4 9–12 (vs. 13–15). It can be distinguished from G. kwangsiensis by (1) MBSR 98–106 (vs. 143–156), (2) VS 31–39 (vs. 41–45), (3) SMC 130–157 (vs. 185–208), (4) DTR 12–15 (vs. 9–11), (5) PP 6–8 (vs. 9–10), (6) LT 1 7–9 (vs. 11–13), (7) IOS 24–28 (vs. 29–31), and (8) LT 4 9–12 (vs. 14–18). It can be distinguished from G. liboensis by (1) MBSR 98–106 (vs. 131–140), (2) SMC 130–157 (vs. 183–195), (3) DTR 12–15 (vs. 9–10), (4) PP 6–8 (vs. 9), (5) LF 1 7–10 (vs. 12–13), (6) LF 4 9–12 (vs. 14–17), (7) LT 1 7–9 (vs. 12–13), (8) IOS 24–28 (vs. 32–35), and (9) LT 4 9–12 (vs. 14–15). Furthermore, it can be distinguished from G. paucituberculatus by (1) MBSR 98–106 (vs. 136–142), (2) VS 31–39 (vs. 42–44), (3) SMC 130–157 (vs. 189–192), (4) DTR 12–15 (vs. 4), (5) PP 6–8 (vs. 12), and (6) IOS 24–28 (vs. 37).
