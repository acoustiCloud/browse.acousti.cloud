diagnosis · Plazi

Diagnosis. Male ( Fig. 1 ). Body black or bluish black with metallic luster. Frons without orbital spots ( Fig. 2 ), but velvety black parafacial spot present below level of antenna ( Fig. 3 ). Frons with pair of posterior orbital setae ( Fig. 2 ). Antennal scape yellowish to pale brown, paler than blackish brown pedicel and flagellum ( Fig. 3 ). One pair of apical scutellar setae present. Femur yellow, the rest legs blackish brown ( Fig. 1 ). Wing infumated, without spots, sometimes darker in distal portion. Female similar to male in external characters except terminalia. Male terminalia: sternum 5 divided into two triangular sclerites ( Fig. 4 ). Cerci with tubercle protuberance subapically ( Fig. 5 ). Surstylus oval in caudal view ( Fig. 6 ). Hypandrium tapering apically ( Fig. 7–8 ). Aedeagal apodeme about as long as ejaculatory apodeme but dilated distally ( Fig. 9 ). Specimens examined. CHINA : Beijing : 1947.
