Distribution Known only from the type locality ( Figure 76 ). MALE (MUSM-ENT 0511135): ( Figures 25–28 ) Total length: 18.94, carapace length 8.14, width 7.02, chelicerae with 10 teeth on promargin. Anterior eye row slightly procurved, posterior eye row slightly recurved. Eye sizes and interdistances: ( Figure 26C ) AME 0.31 (circular), ALE 0.34 (oval), PME 0.25 (oval), PLE 0.26 (oval), AME-AME 0.16, AME-ALE 0.05, PME-PME 0.55, PME-PLE 0.05, ALE-PLE 0.14, AME-PME 0.09, OQ length 0.65, width 1.21. Ocular tubercle length 0.96, width 1.21, clypeus absent. Fovea transverse, procurved, width 1.0, 5.2 from anterior edge of carapace. Labium length 0.95, width 1.40, anterior half with 16 cuspules, maxillae with 107–111 cuspules anteriorly in basal half, short spiniform setae in apical half absent. Sternum length 3.96, width 3.40, three pairs of sternal sigilla located near coxae III (length 0.26, 0.39 from edge of sternum), coxae II (length 0.18, 0.18 from edge of sternum) and coxae I (length 0.10, 0.22 from edge of sternum). Leg formula: IV> I> II> III. Incrassate leg segments: incrassate femur III. Maxillary and trochanteral stridulatory bristles absent. Scopulae: All tarsi 100% densely scopulate, metatarsi I 90%, metatarsi II 60%, metatarsi III 40%, metatarsi IV 20% scopulate. Tarsal scopulae I undivided, tarsal scopulae II divided by a longitudinal line of setae, tarsal scopulae III–IV divided by a longitudinal band of setae. Dorsal face of all tarsi and cymbium with two irregular longitudinal rows of very short claviform trichobothria. Denticulation of paired tarsal claws on left leg (prolateral/ retrolateral row): I 3/3, II 3/4, III 5/3, IV 3/? Plumose setae on retrolateral face of femur IV absent. Palpal organ as in Figure 28 , embolus short and stout with four keels, PS, PI, A and SA keel, PI keel is subtriangular. A keel is short, transparent, SA keel is long and ending on tegulum. Tegulum with short basal apophysis. Retrolateral lobe of cymbium covered with short spiniform setae. Retrolateral face of palpal tibia with indistinct subapical protuberance, covered with spiniform setae ( Figure 26F, G ). Two unequal subapical apophyses are present on tibia I ( Figure 27 ): a longer retrolateral tibial apophysis with long apical spine, a shorter prolateral tibial apophysis with single, retrolateral spine reaching the apex. Metatarsus I not sigmoidly curved and without basal or median protuberance on retrolateral face. When flexed, metatarsus I contacts the retrolateral side of retrolateral tibial apophysis. Abdomen: ( Figure 26E ) urticating setae are located in a central patch (bare in male holotype ). Size of the patch: length 3.30, width 3.05. PLS: length 3.04, basal segment 1.25, middle segment 0.78, apical segment 1.01, all digitiform. PMS: 0.78. Colouration and covering setae: dorsal view: ( Figure 25 ) carapace and coxae brown, covered with golden pubescence, chelicerae and trochantera brown, femora dark grey, patellae, tibiae, metatarsi and tarsi brown. Patellae I, II and palpal patella with two equal longitudinal stripes without covering setae, patellae III, IV with two unequal diagonal stripes without covering setae. Abdomen dark brown, except for the central patch with missing urticating setae which is yellowish. Ventral view: labium and maxillae reddish-brown, sternum, coxae, trochantera brown, femora dark grey, patellae, tibiae, metatarsi and tarsi brown. Abdomen yellowish brown. Spinnerets brown. Ocular tubercle: length 1.74, width 2.05, clypeus absent. Fovea transverse, oval, deep, width 2.38, 9.87 from anterior edge of carapace. Labium length 2.13, width 2.34, anterior quarter with 30 cuspules, maxillae with 122–136 cuspules in basal half, short spiniform setae in apical half absent. Sternum length 7.13, width 5.90, with three visible pairs of sternal sigilla located near coxae III (length 0.48, 0.96 from edge of sternum), coxae II (length 0.46, 0.52 from edge of sternum) and coxae I (length 0.26, 0.65 from edge of sternum). Leg formula: IV> I> II> III. All leg segments uniform. Scopulae: All tarsi 100% densely scopulate, metatarsi I 100%, metatarsi II 70%, metatarsi III 35%, metatarsi IV 25% scopulate. Tarsal scopulae I undivided, tarsal scopulae II, III divided by a longitudinal band of setae, in tarsi IV divided by a wide band of such setae. Dorsal face of all tarsi and cymbium with two irregular longitudinal rows of very short claviform trichobothria. Denticulation of paired tarsal claws on right leg (prolateral/retrolateral row): I 4/4, II 2/2, III 7/7, IV 6/6. Teeth on tarsal claws of anterior legs less developed. Plumose setae on retrolateral face of femur IV absent. Spination: femora I, II p 0-0-1, femur III r 0-0-1, femur IV 0 and femora of palps p 0-0-1, patellae I, II 0, patellae III p 0-0-2, r 1-1-0, patellae IV p 0-1-0 and patellae of palps 0, tibiae I v 1-0-2 (apical), p 1-1-0, II v 1-1-3 (apical), p 1-0-0, III v 1-1-2 (apical), p 1-2-1, r 1-1-0, IV v 2-2-3 (apical), p 1-1-0, r 1-1-0 and tibiae of palps v 1-2-4 (apical), p 0-1-0, metatarsi I v 2-1-1 (apical), p 1-0-0, II v 2-1-2 (apical), p 1-1-0, III v 1-1-2 (apical), p 2-3-2-1, r 2-1-1, IV v 3-2-1-3 (apical), p 1-1-1, r 1-2-1-1, tarsi I–IV and tarsi of palps 0. Spermathecae: ( Figure 32B ) two separated seminal receptacles with distinct granulation, sclerotised basal extension absent. Abdomen: urticating setae of type III are located in the subcircular central patch. PLS: length 5.17, basal segment 2.30, middle segment 1.35, apical segment 1.52, all digitiform. PMS: 1.43. Colouration and covering setae: dorsal view: ( Figure 29 ) carapace, coxae and trochantera brown with golden pubescence, chelicerae brown, femora, patellae, tibiae, metatarsi and tarsi brown, intermixed with long, pale setae. Patellae I, II, palpal patellae, tibiae I–IV, palpal tibiae with two equal parallel longitudinal stripes without covering setae, in tibiae I, II less visible, patellae III, IV with two unequal diagonal stripes without covering setae. Abdomen ( Figure 29 ) covered with short dark brown setae, intermixed with long, rufous setae, except for the oval central patch which is light brown. Length of the central patch: 4.9, width 4.7. Ventral view: maxillae, labium reddish-brown, sternum, coxae, trochantera, femora, patellae, tibiae and metatarsi brown. Abdomen ventrally without dark longitudinal band. Spinnerets brown.
