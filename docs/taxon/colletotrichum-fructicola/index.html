Associated with spathe rot, spadix rot, and leaf spots of Anthurium andraeanum in Sri Lanka ( Vithanage et al. 2021 ); leaf spots of Castanea sp. , leaf blight of Hedychium sp. , and leaf blotches of Rhododendron sp. in Thailand (this study). Colletotrichum fructicola was also reported from Fragaria × ananassa in Canada and the USA ( Weir et al. 2012 ) and Morus alba in China but showed no pathogenicity ( Xue et al. 2019 ). Furthermore, it was reported to cause diseases on Vernicia montana , Cinnamomum camphora , Paulownia fortunei , and Schima superba in China ( Sui et al. 2024 ). Pathogenic on a nematode in China ; infects horsehair worms ( Chordodes formosanus ), a parasite of praying mantises ( De Vivo et al. 2021 ). Pathogenic on humans ; causes Colletotrichum keratitis, a fungal infection of human eyes ( Hung et al. 2020 ). Endophytic on Tetragastris panamensis and Theobroma cacao in Panama ( Weir et al. 2012 ), Cymbopogon citratus and Pennisetum purpureum in Thailand ( Manamgoda et al. 2013 ), Licania tomentosa in Brazil ( Lisboa et al. 2018 ), Dendrobium spp. in China ( Ma et al. 2018 ), Coffea arabica in Thailand ( Numponsak et al. 2018 ), and Magnolia candolli in China ( De Silva et al. 2021 ).
