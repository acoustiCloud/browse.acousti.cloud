description · Plazi

Description. Male: Medium sized, green species, pronotum inflated; stridulatory field length 3.45 mm and width 1.50mm ; hind wing 4.50–5.50mm longer than tegmina; stridulatory file small sub-straight, with about 115 teeth at base; 9th tergite rather weakly projecting behind, apex truncate to very slightly concave; supra-anal plate with lateral margins concave, surface shallowly depressed, apex sub-truncate, apico-lateral angles slightly protruding; cerci short, sub-straight behind basal curvation with a baso-internal black or brown marked swelling, internal margin with a short black or brown marked dilation in basal half; subgenital plate lateral lobes with apical part slightly widening apically, apex truncate; phallus sclerite blackish, elongate and somewhat irregular, lying directly on phallus, only tip free, base forked and hyaline. Female: Ovipositor short, sickle-shaped, dorsal and ventral margins rounded, serrate in apical part; supra-anal plate nearly semicircular with an anchor shaped baso-medial furrow; cerci almost suddenly narrowing sub-apically, apex sub-obtuse, dorsal view slightly curved; sub-genital plate triangular with a longitudinal fold, apex rounded.
